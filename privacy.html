Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last updated: April 2026

Overview

Nāgarika Dhvani helps Bengaluru citizens report civic issues by identifying ward, corporation, and constituency from a photo and location, then opening WhatsApp to the centralized grievance desk.

Data Privacy

We Do NOT Store Your Data

  • No user data collection: We do not collect, store, or retain any personal information, user accounts, or identifiable data.
  • No image storage: Photos you upload are processed in your browser. We do not store images on our servers.
  • No location tracking: GPS coordinates are extracted from your photos or selected on the map, used only to build your report, and not stored by us.
  • AI processing: Images are processed in your browser before you send your report. Face blurring runs locally in your browser for privacy.

WhatsApp Reporting

Important: When you submit a report:

  • Your complaint text (including location and ward details) is sent via WhatsApp to the configured grievance number
  • You review and tap Send in WhatsApp — nothing is sent without your confirmation
  • Photos are attached by you in WhatsApp when prompted; we may download a copy to your device to attach
  • Once sent, the message is governed by WhatsApp's terms and privacy policy

Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage only for:

  • Theme preference (light/dark mode)
  • Language preference (English/Kannada)

No tracking cookies or analytics are used.

User Consent

By using Nāgarika Dhvani, you acknowledge and agree that:

  • Images and location data you submit will be included in your WhatsApp message to the grievance desk
  • You have the right to photograph and share the reported issue
  • You will not share images containing sensitive personal information
  • You are responsible for the content you send

Security

  • All connections use HTTPS encryption
  • Image compression and face blurring happen in your browser
  • WhatsApp opens on your device; we do not access your WhatsApp account

Liability

Nāgarika Dhvani is provided "as is" without warranties. We are not responsible for:

  • Response times or actions taken by authorities
  • Content you send via WhatsApp
  • Third-party services (WhatsApp, map providers)

Contact

For questions or concerns, reach out via WhatsApp to the grievance desk at 9480685700.
