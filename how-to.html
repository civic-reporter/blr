How to Use

Quick guide to reporting civic issues in Bengaluru

Reporting a Civic Issue

  1. Navigate to Civic Issues from the home page
  2. Click Mobile Camera (on mobile) or Upload Photo
  3. Allow GPS detection or manually set location on map
  4. Confirm that issue is seen on the photo
  5. Select issue category (pothole, drainage, etc.)
  6. Add optional details
  7. Review ward, corporation, and constituency details, then click Send via WhatsApp

Tips for Civic Reporting

  • Take clear photos showing the issue
  • Ensure your GPS is enabled for accurate location
  • Select the most relevant issue category
  • Reports must be within Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) limits

Using the Map

If your photo doesn't have GPS data or you need to adjust the location:

  1. Look for the map that appears after uploading your photo
  2. Click on the map to place a marker at the exact location
  3. Or use the search bar to find a location by address
  4. Drag the marker to fine-tune the position
  5. The location must be within GBA/Bengaluru boundaries

Need Help?

If you encounter any issues or have questions:

  • Check that you're within Bengaluru city limits
  • Ensure your photo clearly shows the issue
  • Make sure you've selected an issue category
  • Verify that your location is set correctly on the map
