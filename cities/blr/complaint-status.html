Check complaint status

Look up GBA or BWSSB complaints by complaint ID or registered mobile number on the Bengaluru Smart City portal.

  1. Select Complaints, then choose GBA or BWSSB (GBA may appear as BBMP on the portal).
  2. Search by Complaint ID or Mobile Number.
  3. Submit on the portal to view status and grievance details.
